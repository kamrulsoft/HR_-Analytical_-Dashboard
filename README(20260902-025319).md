# 📊 HR Analysis Dashboard

![HR Analysis Dashboard](hr-analysis-dashboard.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

The **HR Analysis Dashboard** is an Excel-based data analytics project designed to transform employee and workforce data into clear, actionable business insights. The dashboard provides an interactive overview of key Human Resources metrics, including employee headcount, gender distribution, salary, employment type, employee satisfaction, education, work experience, and performance ratings.

This project demonstrates practical **Data Analyst skills** in:

- Data cleaning and preparation
- HR data analysis
- KPI development
- Excel dashboard design
- Data visualization
- Trend and comparison analysis
- Business insight generation

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The purpose of this dashboard is to help HR teams and decision-makers better understand the organization's workforce by answering questions such as:

- How many employees are currently in the organization?
- What is the gender distribution of employees?
- What is the total salary cost?
- How does salary vary by month?
- What is the distribution of full-time and contract employees?
- Which education levels are most common among employees?
- How are employees distributed by years of work experience?
- What is the employee satisfaction level?
- How do performance ratings differ by gender?

## 2. Total Current Employees

**1,233 Employees**

## 3. Total Salary Analysis

The dashboard reports the total salary amount:

**8,424,768**

This metric helps management monitor overall employee compensation costs and supports workforce budgeting.

## 4. Salary Trend by Month

A monthly line chart visualizes salary trends from **January through December**.

This analysis can help identify:

- High and low salary periods

The visualization shows that the full-time workforce represents the larger share of salary expenditure compared with contract employees.
